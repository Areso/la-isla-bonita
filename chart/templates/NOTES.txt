la-isla-bonita is deploying to namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Play from inside the pod (client is in the image):

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec -it deploy/{{ include "isla.fullname" . }} -- isla

Or reach it from your machine:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "isla.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl -X POST localhost:8080/world/init

Then run the client against it: ISLA_SERVER=http://localhost:8080
